What Is Kakobuy?

Kakobuy is a Chinese shopping agent. It buys items for you from marketplaces like Taobao, Weidian and 1688, stores them in its warehouse, lets you inspect them, then forwards everything to your country in one parcel.

The part shoppers care about most is quality control. When your item reaches the warehouse, staff photograph the actual product — stitching, logos, materials, colour — so you review QC photos before paying for international shipping.

In short: Kakobuy gives you access to products and prices you can't reach on Western sites, with a QC step that protects you and shipping options that protect your budget.

Is Kakobuy safe and legit?

Kakobuy is an established Chinese shopping agent — a paid intermediary that buys items on your behalf from Taobao, Weidian and 1688. Every order runs through Kakobuy's systems with secured payments, tracking, and warehouse QC photos. You're responsible for what you order and for reviewing QC before approving shipment.

How much does Kakobuy shipping cost?

It depends on weight, dimensions, and which shipping line you choose. Use Kakobuy's shipping estimator before you approve shipment. Removing bulky packaging or consolidating items into one parcel can cut the total.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends entirely on the shipping line you pick — express is faster and costs more; economy is cheaper and slower. Kakobuy provides a tracking number once your parcel ships.
